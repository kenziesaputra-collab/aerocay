Artisanal Craftsmanship, Certified Halal Trust

Founded on generations of butchery knowledge, Boucherie Lenggana brings upscale European deli culture and strict halal standards together under one roof. Every piece of meat is carefully selected from ethically managed farms with full traceability.

We believe that meat preparation is an art form. From our custom dry-aging cabinet to our handmade sausages and spice blends, we preserve timeless techniques to ensure superior taste, tenderness, and satisfaction for your family table.
