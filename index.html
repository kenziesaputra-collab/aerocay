Frequently Asked Questions

Find detailed answers regarding our Halal certification, cold-chain delivery, custom butchery options, and storage advice.

All our meats, including imported Wagyu, Australian lamb, and French charcuterie items, are 100% Halal certified by recognized international bodies (such as JAKIM, MUI, and AUS-MEAT Halal). Slaughtering follows strict Zabiha humane guidelines, and no non-halal items ever enter our facilities.
Every single order is custom sliced, vacuum-sealed in food-grade airtight pouching, and packed in thermal insulated cooling bags with gel ice packs. We deliver via instant cold-chain couriers across Greater Jakarta (Jabodetabek) and overnight express cold freight for other major Indonesian cities.
Absolutely. As an artisanal butchery, customization is our pride. You can specify exact steak thickness (e.g., 1 inch, 2 inch), bone trimming, de-boning, or custom dry-aging duration in our salt-lined aging chambers when placing your order via WhatsApp.
Unopened vacuum-sealed fresh chilled meat remains in optimal condition for 3 to 5 days in a standard refrigerator (0°C to 4°C). If you plan to store it longer, place the vacuum pack directly into the freezer (-18°C), where it maintains peak quality for up to 6 months.
