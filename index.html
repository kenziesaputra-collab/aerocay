Our Story

The Heritage of Boucherie Lenggana

Founded on the principles of classic Parisian boucherie culture and refined gourmet standards, Boucherie Lenggana represents the pinnacle of ethical, artisanal meat production.

Our founder, trained under master artisans in Lyon and Paris, recognized the growing desire for uncompromising quality in the Halal culinary sphere. By partnering exclusively with small-batch heritage farms that prioritize animal welfare, organic feeding, and stress-free environments, we deliver unmatched meat tenderness and rich flavor.

Whether you require delicate Wagyu marbling for a festive celebration or dry-aged roasts for an extraordinary dinner, our master butchers cut each piece to order with pride and artistry.
